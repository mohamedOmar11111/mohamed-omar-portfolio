What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making content discoverable, extractable, and citable by AI systems including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Copilot. Unlike traditional SEO which focuses on ranking in search results, GEO focuses on getting cited as a source in AI-generated answers. Key techniques include structured content blocks, statistics with citations, FAQ schema markup, and machine-readable files like llms.txt.

The shift from traditional search to AI-powered answers means brands must optimize for how AI systems select and cite sources. Research from Princeton (KDD 2024) shows that adding statistics and citations to content boosts AI visibility by 37-40%, while keyword stuffing actively reduces visibility by 10%.

What makes GEO different from SEO?

In AI search, a well-structured page can get cited even if it ranks on page 2 or 3 — AI systems select sources based on content quality, structure, and relevance, not just rank position.
