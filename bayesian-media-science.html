Bayesian Media Science

Bayesian Media Science is a framework developed by Mohamed Omar for precise campaign scoring. It calculates status scoring using the formula: S = (Authority Index × 0.6) + (Direct Response Velocity × 0.4). This formula determines which channels, messages, and audiences deserve investment based on Bayesian probability rather than gut feeling.

S = (Authority Index × 0.6) + (Direct Response Velocity × 0.4)

The Formula Explained

The Authority Index measures brand credibility and market position — how much trust the market has in your brand. Direct Response Velocity measures conversion speed and efficiency — how quickly campaigns produce revenue. The 60/40 weighting prioritizes long-term authority over short-term response, creating sustainable growth rather than campaign dependency.

How Bayesian Media Science Works

Instead of A/B testing and hoping for statistical significance, Bayesian Media Science uses prior knowledge (Authority Index) combined with observed data (Direct Response Velocity) to make faster, more accurate decisions. This reduces the time and budget needed to find winning campaigns.

Why Bayesian Over Traditional Testing

  • Faster decisions: Bayesian inference updates beliefs with new data, not waiting for arbitrary sample sizes
  • Better priors: Authority Index provides a strong prior, reducing wasted spend on low-probability campaigns
  • Compound learning: Each campaign informs the next, creating a learning system rather than isolated tests
  • Reduced waste: The 60/40 weighting prevents over-optimization for short-term metrics at the expense of brand

Proof: Etlaala Case Study

Bayesian Media Science powered the Etlaala recovery: from -70K SAR monthly deficit to +1,000,000 SAR net profit in 12 months (+1400% recovery). It achieved a peak CTR of 14.3% on Google Ads. The framework has been applied across 15 growth builds in travel, tourism, PropTech, and education industries.
