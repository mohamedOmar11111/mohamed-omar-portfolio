# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

MENA and GCC founders, CEOs, growth leaders, and commercial decision-makers reviewing Mohamed Omar before a high-stakes strategy conversation. They need to establish credibility, judgment, regional fluency, and evidence quickly.

## Product Purpose

Position Mohamed Omar as The Growth Architect: a revenue systems engineer who diagnoses growth leaks and designs durable acquisition infrastructure. The primary conversion is a qualified request to book a strategy call.

## Brand Personality

Forensic. Sovereign. Precise. The experience should feel controlled, rigorous, and unmistakably authored—more boardroom evidence dossier than marketing portfolio.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-tool landing pages, hacker-terminal cosplay, neon-on-black cyberpunk, decorative glassmorphism, agency card grids, unsupported performance claims, and inflated corporate language.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before assertion.
- Make judgment visible, not just outcomes.
- One decisive conversion path: request a strategy call.
- Regional authority without cultural clichés.
- Distinctive composition, accessible execution.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior. Keep the English reading level direct and internationally clear.
