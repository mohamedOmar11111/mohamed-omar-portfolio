What is Growth Architecture?

Growth Architecture is a methodology that treats business growth as an engineering problem rather than a marketing campaign problem. It connects market signal, positioning, acquisition, and follow-through into one system so revenue does not depend on heroic human effort. Unlike traditional marketing agencies that sell inputs — hours, clicks, impressions — Growth Architecture installs permanent revenue infrastructure: workflows, agents, content nodes, and reporting layers that continue operating after the engagement ends.

Growth Architect vs Fractional CMO — what is the difference?

A Fractional CMO manages the marketing function a business already has. A Growth Architect re-engineers the revenue kernel itself — installing permanent, AI-driven infrastructure instead of running another round of campaigns. The Growth Architect approach focuses on building systems that scale without increasing headcount, while a Fractional CMO typically optimizes existing team structures and processes.

What is Onyx Hive?

Onyx Hive is a multi-agent AI framework comprising 36 specialized agents organized in three tiers: Sensory Layer (crawlers, trend detectors, dialect mapping), Cognitive Layer (scenario analysis, content planning, strategy auditing), and Execution Layer (automatic deployment, ad account budget shifts, self-healing codebases). It operates on a low-burn resource throttle capped at 20% threshold rate-limits to maintain operational stability.
