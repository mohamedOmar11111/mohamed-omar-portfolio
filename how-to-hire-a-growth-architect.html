How to Hire a Growth Architect

To hire a Growth Architect, first diagnose whether your problem is architecture or execution. If your ads work but revenue doesn't move, you have an architecture problem. Look for proof — case studies with specific numbers, not generic testimonials. Evaluate the methodology — it should focus on infrastructure, not campaigns. Start with a diagnostic before committing to a full engagement.

Step-by-Step Guide

01

Diagnose the Problem

Before hiring a Growth Architect, identify whether your problem is architecture or execution. If your ads work, your agency is competent, but revenue doesn't move — you have an architecture problem. If you need more hands on deck — you have an execution problem. Growth Architecture solves architecture problems, not capacity problems.

02

Check for Proof

Look for case studies with specific numbers — revenue recovery, cost per lead, conversion improvements. A credible Growth Architect will show verified results, not generic testimonials. Ask for the methodology behind the numbers. The Etlaala case study shows +1M SAR net profit recovery with a +1400% recovery rate.

03

Evaluate the Methodology

A real Growth Architect has a defined methodology — not just frameworks but systems. Ask: What is your diagnostic process? How do you measure success? What happens after the engagement ends? The answer should focus on infrastructure, not campaigns. Growth Architecture installs permanent revenue systems that operate after the engagement.

04

Assess Regional Fit

For MENA markets, ask about Arabic dialect vectorization, cultural intent mapping, and multi-market precision. A Growth Architect serving Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt should understand that these markets behave differently despite looking similar on a media plan. Regional precision beats translated templates.

05

Start with a Diagnostic

Begin with a Forensic Revenue Audit — a structured consultation mapping systemic leakages in your conversion loops. This diagnostic should reveal the structural constraint before any system is designed. A credible Growth Architect diagnoses before prescribing. The audit should take 1-2 weeks.

Questions to Ask

  • What is your diagnostic process?
  • Can you show case studies with specific numbers?
  • What happens after the engagement ends?
  • How do you measure success?
  • Do you have experience in my market?
  • What is your approach to AI integration?
  • How do you handle Arabic dialect vectorization?
  • What is your Onyx Hive framework?

Red Flags

  • No case studies with specific numbers
  • Focus on campaigns instead of infrastructure
  • No diagnostic process — jumping straight to solutions
  • Generic templates instead of market-specific approaches
  • No clear handoff plan — creating dependency
  • Guaranteeing outcomes without access to operating data

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a Growth Architect?

You need a Growth Architect when growth activity is high but revenue logic is fragmented. When your ads work, your agency is competent, but the revenue still doesn't move. When you want infrastructure your team can operate — not dependency on an agency.

What is a Forensic Revenue Audit?

A Forensic Revenue Audit is a structured consultation that maps systemic leakages in your conversion loops. It audits the revenue path, message, media, data, and follow-through — separating symptoms from the structural constraint. It is the first step in Growth Architecture.

How long does a Growth Architecture engagement take?

A Forensic Revenue Audit takes 1-2 weeks. A full Growth Architecture installation typically takes 3-6 months. The Etlaala case study took 12 months of system optimization. The goal is to install infrastructure that operates permanently.
