The Problem

Etlaala Travel & Tourism, Saudi Arabia's premier travel platform, was operating at a -70,000 SAR monthly deficit. A 5-person marketing team was running campaigns but the revenue logic was fragmented. The ads were competent, but the system was not. Growth activity was high, but revenue was declining.

The Solution: Growth Architecture

Mohamed Omar deployed a three-phase Growth Architecture approach:

  • Bayesian Media Science: Calculated precise status scoring using S = (Authority Index × 0.6) + (Direct Response Velocity × 0.4)
  • Agentic Intake Swarms: Automated lead classification and follow-through systems
  • Multi-Channel Acquisition: Google Ads, Meta Ads, Snapchat, and TikTok with AI-driven intent classification
  • Arabic Dialect Vectorization: Localized content for Saudi and Egyptian markets using MSA and Gulf Ammiya

The Result

In 12 months, Etlaala recovered from a -70K SAR monthly deficit to +1,000,000 SAR net profit — a +1400% recovery. The AI-driven system achieved a peak CTR of 14.3% and replaced the need for a 5-person human marketing team with permanent, autonomous infrastructure.

What is Etlaala?

Etlaala Travel & Tourism (etlaala.com) is Saudi Arabia's premier travel platform headquartered in Mecca. It offers flights, hotels, cruises, car rentals, Hajj/Umrah packages, and Saudi outbound tourism services. The company has 20-30 employees and covers Saudi Arabia (78%) and Egypt (22%).

Can Growth Architecture work for my business?

Growth Architecture works for businesses with real demand, operational complexity, and a costly commercial constraint. It is most effective for eCommerce and SaaS companies in the MENA region — Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt — that have high growth activity but fragmented revenue logic.
